MISSION

The finished paper and current thesis describe the same CliniCause project but prioritize different scientific stories.

The finished paper’s main idea is that CliniCause constructs clinically grounded observational causal-analysis testbeds from source-observed irregular ICU records without generating patient records, proxy-exposure assignments, or mortality outcomes. It shifts researcher intervention from the data-generating mechanism to an explicit representation layer and evaluates the resulting resources through converging evidence rather than known causal ground truth.

The current thesis instead primarily emphasizes end-to-end integration, data contracts, traceability, and evidence tracking.

The thesis is already detailed and scientifically cautious. It must not be rewritten from scratch. The eventual revision will make observational testbed construction the primary contribution while preserving integration and evidence tracking as the enabling machinery.
